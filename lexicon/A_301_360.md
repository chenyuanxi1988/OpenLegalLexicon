## Article 102
- **English definition:** Article 102 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, which prohibits one or more undertakings holding a dominant position in the internal market or a substantial part of it from abusing that position insofar as the conduct may affect trade between EU member states. Dominance itself is not prohibited; abusive exploitation or exclusion is.
- **中文译名:** 《欧盟运行条约》第102条；滥用市场支配地位禁止条款
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU competition law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-262`
- **Review status:** drafted

## articles of association
- **English definition:** A company’s internal constitutional rules governing matters such as directors’ powers, decision-making, shares, member voting, meetings, and other aspects of corporate administration. Under UK company law the articles form part of the company’s constitution and bind the company and its members in the statutory manner.

## artificial nutrition and hydration
- **English definition:** Clinically assisted provision of nutrition and fluids, for example through feeding tubes or other medical techniques, to a person unable to take sufficient food or fluid orally. It is treated as medical treatment; for a patient lacking capacity, provision or withdrawal must be decided according to the Mental Capacity Act 2005 best-interests framework and any valid advance decision or authorized decision-maker. Court involvement is not automatically required in every case, but may be necessary where there is disagreement, uncertainty, or another issue requiring judicial determination.
- **中文译名:** 人工营养与补液；临床辅助营养和水分供给

- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4383`
- **Review status:** drafted; post-2018 court-approval position reflected

- **English definition:** In criminal law, involuntary conduct occurring without the defendant’s conscious control. **Non-insane automatism** may provide a complete defence where the involuntary state results from an external cause and the legal conditions are met; automatism attributable to an internal condition may instead fall within the law of insanity.

## autrefois acquit
- **English definition:** French for “previously acquitted”. A special plea asserting that the accused has already been finally acquitted of the same or substantially the same offence and therefore cannot ordinarily be tried again. Statute creates limited exceptions permitting retrial after acquittal for specified serious offences where strict conditions, including new and compelling evidence, are met.
- **中文译名:** 曾经无罪；先前无罪抗辩；一罪不二审抗辩
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal procedure / double jeopardy
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-312`

## auxiliary jurisdiction
- **English definition:** Historical equitable jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery used to assist proceedings at common law, for example by compelling discovery or preserving evidence where the common-law courts lacked adequate powers. The procedural need for this separate jurisdiction disappeared after the Judicature reforms fused administration of law and equity.
