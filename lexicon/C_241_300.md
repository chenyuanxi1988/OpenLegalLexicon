## collective redundancy
- **English definition:** Proposed dismissal for redundancy of a group of employees large enough to trigger the statutory collective-consultation regime. In Great Britain, the current core threshold remains a proposal to dismiss as redundant **20 or more employees at one establishment within a period of 90 days or less**, requiring consultation with appropriate employee representatives and notification to the Secretary of State. From 6 April 2026, the maximum protective award for failure to comply with collective consultation duties is 180 days’ pay. Further organisation-wide threshold reforms under the Employment Rights Act 2025 are being implemented separately and should not be treated as already replacing the existing establishment test unless commenced.
- **中文译名:** 集体裁员；集体冗员解雇
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Great Britain; employment / redundancy law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-673`
- **Review status:** drafted

More generally, a group of persons linked by locality, identity, interest, or another shared characteristic, where a statute or legal rule gives the concept significance.
- **中文译名:** 1. 威尔士基层社区行政区 2. 社区；共同体
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Wales local government / general law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-723`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Community dimension
- **English definition:** Term in EU merger-control law for a concentration meeting the turnover thresholds that bring it within the jurisdiction of the EU Merger Regulation rather than, subject to referral mechanisms, solely within national merger-control systems. Modern legislation more commonly speaks of a concentration having an **EU dimension**.
- **中文译名:** 欧盟层面的规模标准；具有欧盟维度的企业集中
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU merger / competition law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-724`
- **Review status:** drafted

## community home
- **English definition:** Older statutory and administrative term for residential accommodation provided for children in local-authority care or under child-welfare arrangements. Modern children’s social-care law uses different categories such as **children’s homes**, secure children’s homes, and other regulated placements, so “community home” is principally historical or appears in residual statutory references.
- **中文译名:** 社区儿童之家；地方当局儿童安置机构（旧称）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; child-care law; historical terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-725`
- **Review status:** drafted

## community interest company
- **English definition:** **CIC**, a limited company registered under the Companies Acts and Community Interest Company regime to carry on activities for the benefit of a community. It must satisfy the statutory **community interest test** and is subject to an **asset lock** restricting extraction or transfer of assets except as permitted by law, together with reporting and oversight by the Regulator of Community Interest Companies.
- **中文译名:** 社区利益公司；公益型社区公司（CIC）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK company / social-enterprise law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4413`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Community law
- **English definition:** Historical expression for the law of the **European Communities**, especially before the Lisbon Treaty. Modern terminology is **European Union law** or **EU law**, encompassing the EU Treaties, Charter within its scope, regulations, directives, decisions, general principles, international agreements, and Court of Justice case law according to their legal status.
- **中文译名:** 欧洲共同体法（旧称）；现通常称欧盟法
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU law; historical terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-726`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Community Legal Service
- **English definition:** Former legal-aid and publicly funded legal-services framework created by the Access to Justice Act 1999 for civil legal help and representation. The statutory Community Legal Service framework and Legal Services Commission arrangements were replaced from **1 April 2013** by the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 regime, administered principally through the **Legal Aid Agency**.
- **中文译名:** 社区法律服务制度（旧英国法律援助体系）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; legal aid; historical since 2013
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-727`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Community legislation
- **English definition:** Historical expression for legislative acts of the European Communities. In modern EU terminology, legislation and other legal acts include **regulations, directives, and decisions** adopted under the EU Treaties, together with delegated and implementing acts where the Treaties and legislation authorize them.
- **中文译名:** 欧洲共同体立法（旧称）；欧盟立法
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU law; historical terminology

## community of assets
- **English definition:** Matrimonial-property system under which some or all property acquired by spouses or civil partners is treated as jointly owned or pooled under a statutory community regime. Such systems exist in many civil-law jurisdictions but are **not the automatic general matrimonial-property regime of England and Wales**, where ownership, trusts, and discretionary family-court redistribution operate differently.
- **中文译名:** 夫妻共同财产制；财产共同体制度
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Comparative family / matrimonial property law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-729`
- **Review status:** drafted

## community order
- **English definition:** Adult sentence under the Sentencing Code requiring an offender to comply with one or more **community requirements** imposed by the court, such as unpaid work, rehabilitation, treatment, curfew, exclusion, residence, alcohol or drug requirements, or other requirements currently authorised by statute. A community order may be imposed only where the statutory seriousness threshold and other sentencing conditions are met. The available requirement menu can change by commencement of amending legislation, so older fixed lists should not be treated as exhaustive.
- **中文译名:** 社区令；社区矫正令；社区刑罚命令
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal sentencing law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-730`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Community Patent
- **English definition:** Historical project for a single patent right covering the European Community or European Union. The original “Community Patent” proposals did not become an operative patent system. Their policy objective was later realised in a different form through the **European patent with unitary effect (Unitary Patent)** and the Unified Patent Court system, launched on **1 June 2023** for participating EU Member States. The United Kingdom withdrew from the UPC Agreement in 2020 and does not participate in the Unitary Patent/UPC system.
- **中文译名:** 欧洲共同体专利（历史项目）；后续发展为欧盟单一专利／Unitary Patent
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU patent law; historical term with current successor regime
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4649`
- **Review status:** drafted

## community patient
- **English definition:** Patient subject to a **Community Treatment Order (CTO)** under the Mental Health Act 1983 after discharge from specified compulsory hospital treatment into the community. The patient remains subject to conditions and recall powers while the CTO is in force and retains statutory rights concerning review, representation, and tribunal proceedings.
- **中文译名:** 社区治疗患者；受社区治疗令约束的患者
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; mental-health law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4414`
- **Review status:** drafted

## community sentence
- **English definition:** Criminal sentence served in the community rather than as immediate custody. For adults, the principal statutory form is the **community order** containing one or more community requirements; suspended sentences can also contain community requirements but remain legally distinct sentencing disposals. For children and young people, the principal community sentence is ordinarily a **youth rehabilitation order** under the youth sentencing regime.
- **中文译名:** 社区刑罚；非监禁社区判刑
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal sentencing law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-733`
- **Review status:** drafted
