## Charity Commission
- **English definition:** The independent statutory regulator and registrar of charities in England and Wales. Its functions include maintaining the register of charities, promoting compliance by charity trustees with their legal duties, providing guidance, conducting inquiries and investigations, and using statutory protective or remedial powers where charity property, administration, or public confidence is at risk.
- **中文译名:** 英格兰及威尔士慈善委员会；慈善委员会
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; charity law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-571`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Charity Tribunal
- **English definition:** Former name for the specialist tribunal created to hear certain appeals, reviews, and references concerning decisions of the Charity Commission. ## Charter of Fundamental Rights
- **English definition:** The **Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union**, which sets out civil, political, economic, and social rights binding on EU institutions and on member states when they act within the scope of EU law. It acquired the same legal value as the EU Treaties when the Lisbon Treaty entered into force in 2009. The Charter itself was not incorporated into general UK domestic law after Brexit; its relevance in the UK is now limited to particular EU-law, Withdrawal Agreement, or other legally preserved contexts rather than operating as a general domestic bill of rights.
- **中文译名:** 《欧盟基本权利宪章》
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU fundamental-rights law / post-Brexit UK context
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-573`
- **Review status:** drafted

## charterparty
- **English definition:** Contract by which a shipowner agrees to make a vessel, or its carrying capacity, available to a charterer for an agreed voyage, period, or other maritime purpose. Principal forms include **voyage charter**, **time charter**, and **bareboat or demise charter**. Allocation of possession, navigation, freight or hire, loading, risk, and liabilities depends on the type and terms of the charterparty.
- **中文译名:** 租船合同；船舶租用合同
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Maritime / carriage law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-574`
- **Review status:** drafted

## chastisement
- **English definition:** Physical punishment used as discipline. In England, the common-law defence of **reasonable punishment** is narrowly limited and is unavailable for specified offences causing or risking more serious harm; corporal punishment in schools is prohibited. Wales abolished the defence of reasonable punishment in 2022, and Scotland has separately abolished the equivalent defence. The legality of physical discipline therefore depends on jurisdiction and the nature of the conduct.
- **中文译名:** 体罚；惩戒性身体处罚
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK child / criminal law; jurisdiction-specific
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-575`
