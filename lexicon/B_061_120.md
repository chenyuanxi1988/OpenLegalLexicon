## Bournewood gap
- **English definition:** The former legal gap identified after the detention of an adult lacking decision-making capacity who did not actively resist admission to hospital and therefore lacked the safeguards of formal Mental Health Act detention. The European Court of Human Rights found the arrangements insufficient under Article 5 ECHR. Parliament responded by creating the **Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS)** within the Mental Capacity Act framework. Although later legislation created Liberty Protection Safeguards, DoLS continue to operate in hospitals and care homes as of 2026.
- **中文译名:** “Bournewood 漏洞”；无行为能力者非正式拘束的程序保障缺口
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; mental capacity / human rights
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4392`
- **Review status:** drafted; 2026 DoLS status corrected

## brewster sessions
- **English definition:** Historical annual sessions of licensing justices dealing with grants, renewals, and transfers of licences for the sale of alcohol. The old justices’ licensing system was displaced by the modern licensing-authority regime under the Licensing Act 2003.
- **中文译名:** 酒类执照年度治安法官会议（历史制度）
