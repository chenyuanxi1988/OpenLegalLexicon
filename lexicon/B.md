## backed for bail

## bail
- **English definition:** Conditional release of a person from custody pending trial, sentence, appeal, or another stage of criminal proceedings, subject to a duty to surrender and any conditions lawfully imposed. Bail may be granted by police or a court and can be refused only on grounds permitted by law.
- **中文译名:** 保释；取保候审（功能近似译法，非制度等同）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Criminal procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-325`
- **Review status:** drafted

## bailee

## Banks v Goodfellow test
- **English definition:** Common-law test of testamentary capacity derived from *Banks v Goodfellow* (1870). In broad terms, a testator must understand the nature and effect of making a will, the general extent of the property disposed of, the claims of persons who might reasonably be considered, and must not be affected by a disorder of mind that poisons the dispositions made.
- **中文译名:** Banks v Goodfellow 遗嘱能力测试；立遗嘱能力标准
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; wills / capacity
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4943`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Beth Din
- **English definition:** A Jewish religious court applying Jewish law to religious-status and private disputes within its recognized sphere. In England its decisions do not displace state law, but parties may in appropriate civil matters agree to use a Beth Din as an arbitral tribunal subject to ordinary arbitration law.
- **中文译名:** 犹太宗教法庭；Beth Din
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Religious law / arbitration / family status
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-374`
- **Review status:** drafted

## betting duty
- **English definition:** Tax imposed on specified betting activities, generally charged by reference to bookmakers’ or betting operators’ profits or other statutory measures rather than simply the gross stakes received. The UK regime contains different duties for general, pool, remote, and other forms of gambling.
- **中文译名:** 博彩税；投注税
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK tax / gambling
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4946`
- **Review status:** drafted

## bias
- **English definition:** Lack, or apparent lack, of impartiality by a judge, tribunal member, public decision-maker, arbitrator, or other person required to decide fairly. A decision may be invalid where actual bias is shown or where the circumstances create the legally sufficient appearance or risk of bias.
- **中文译名:** 偏见；偏私；不公正倾向
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Administrative / judicial / arbitration procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-375`
