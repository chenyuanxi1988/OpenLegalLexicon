## annus et dies
- **English definition:** Latin for “a year and a day”. In medieval common law the expression was associated with the Crown’s former right, following felony, to take the offender’s lands and profits for a year and a day before forfeiture consequences operated.
- **中文译名:** 一年零一天；“年、日及耗损权”（历史普通法语境）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Historical common law; legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-210`
- **Review status:** drafted

## answer

In many modern procedures the equivalent document is called a **defence**, **response**, or **acknowledgment and response**.
- **中文译名:** 答辩；答辩书；对申请／呈请的正式回应
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Civil / family / tribunal procedure; terminology varies by procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-211`
- **Review status:** drafted

## antecedents

## anticipatory breach
- **English definition:** A repudiatory breach occurring before contractual performance is due, where a party clearly indicates that it will not perform or disables itself from performing an essential obligation. - **中文译名:** 预期违约；先期违约

## anticipatory self-defence
- **English definition:** Use of force claimed as self-defence against an armed attack said to be imminent but not yet underway. Its permissibility under international law is contested: Article 51 of the UN Charter expressly refers to self-defence if an armed attack occurs, while some states and commentators argue that customary international law permits action against an attack that is truly imminent and satisfies necessity and proportionality.
- **中文译名:** 预先自卫；预期性自卫
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international law; use of force
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-216`
- **Review status:** drafted

## anticompetitive practice

- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-217`

## Article 50
- **English definition:** Article 50 of the Treaty on European Union, which establishes the process by which an EU member state may decide, in accordance with its constitutional requirements, to withdraw from the Union, notify the European Council, and negotiate arrangements for withdrawal. The United Kingdom used Article 50 for Brexit and ceased to be an EU member state on 31 January 2020.
- **中文译名:** 《欧洲联盟条约》第50条；欧盟成员国退出条款
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** EU constitutional law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4824`
- **Review status:** drafted; UK withdrawal status updated

## Article 101
- **English definition:** Article 101 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, prohibiting agreements between undertakings, decisions by associations, and concerted practices that may affect trade between EU member states and have as their object or effect the prevention, restriction, or distortion of competition, subject to the conditions for exemption in Article 101(3). Agreements prohibited by Article 101(1) and not exempt are void to the extent provided by EU law.
- **中文译名:** 《欧盟运行条约》第101条；反竞争协议禁止条款
