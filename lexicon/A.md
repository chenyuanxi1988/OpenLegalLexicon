# OpenLegalLexicon — A

> Oxford 10e source order. English definitions below are independently drafted; Chinese gives term-level translation rather than line-by-line translation of Oxford prose.

## abandonment
- **English definition:** 1. Intentional relinquishment of a legal right or interest, especially ownership or possession; in marine insurance, surrender of the insured interest may follow a constructive total loss. 2. Giving up all or part of a claim, appeal, or proceeding. 3. In child-protection usage, leaving a child without proper care or responsibility.
- **中文译名:** 1. 放弃；抛弃（权利、所有权或财产权益） 2. 撤回／放弃诉讼请求或上诉 3. 遗弃儿童
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Common law; England and Wales; marine insurance / civil procedure / family law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-1`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abatement
- **English definition:** Reduction, suspension, or extinction of a legal claim, payment, nuisance, or legacy. In estate administration it particularly means reduction of testamentary gifts when the estate is insufficient to satisfy them in full; in debt contexts it may mean proportionate reduction of amounts payable.
- **中文译名:** 减缩；减额；消减；遗产法中常指遗赠减缩
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Common law; succession / debt / remedies
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-2`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abduction
- **English definition:** Unlawfully taking away or detaining another person, commonly by force, threat, deception, or without lawful authority. The precise offence depends on the victim and circumstances, including child abduction and kidnapping.
- **中文译名:** 绑架；诱拐；非法带走或扣留他人
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-3`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abet
- **English definition:** To encourage, support, or assist another person in committing an offence; commonly used with “aid, abet, counsel or procure” in rules on secondary participation.
- **中文译名:** 教唆；怂恿；协助犯罪
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4`
- **Review status:** drafted

## ABH
- **English definition:** Abbreviation for **actual bodily harm**, bodily injury more than merely transient or trifling in the English offence of assault occasioning actual bodily harm.
- **中文译名:** actual bodily harm 的缩写；实际身体伤害
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-5`
- **Review status:** drafted

## ab initio
- **English definition:** Latin for “from the beginning”; used where a legal status, transaction, or defect is treated as existing from the outset rather than arising only later.
- **中文译名:** 自始；从一开始
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** General legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-6`
- **Review status:** drafted

## ab intestato
- **English definition:** Latin describing succession from a person who dies without leaving a valid will, so that the estate passes under rules of intestacy.
- **中文译名:** 无遗嘱继承；依无遗嘱规则继承
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Succession; legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4358`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abortion
- **English definition:** Termination of a pregnancy before birth. Its lawfulness, permitted grounds, procedure, and criminal consequences are jurisdiction-specific; in Great Britain the framework includes the Abortion Act 1967 and related criminal law.
- **中文译名:** 堕胎；人工终止妊娠
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Great Britain; medical / criminal law; Northern Ireland has a distinct regime
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-7`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absconding
- **English definition:** Leaving, hiding, or failing to surrender in order to evade arrest, detention, court proceedings, or another legal obligation; in criminal procedure commonly used for failure to surrender to custody.
- **中文译名:** 潜逃；逃匿；为逃避司法程序而不到案
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Criminal procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-8`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absence
- **English definition:** Non-attendance by a party, defendant, witness, or other required person at a hearing or trial. Consequences depend on the proceeding and may include adjournment, judgment, dismissal, or proceeding in the person’s absence where permitted.
- **中文译名:** 缺席；不到庭
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Civil and criminal procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-9`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absente reo
- **English definition:** Latin meaning “the defendant being absent”, describing proceedings or acts taking place without the accused or defendant present.
- **中文译名:** 被告缺席；在被告不在场的情况下
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Legal Latin; procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4359`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absent-mindedness
- **English definition:** A temporary failure of attention or awareness. In criminal law it matters only where the evidence supports a recognized doctrine such as automatism; ordinary forgetfulness does not itself excuse liability.
- **中文译名:** 心不在焉；疏忽性失神
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-10`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absent parent
- **English definition:** Older child-support terminology for a parent who does not live with the child; modern UK usage generally prefers **non-resident parent** or equivalent terminology.
- **中文译名:** 非同住父母；未与子女共同居住的父母（旧称）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK; child support; historical terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-11`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute
- **English definition:** Complete, unconditional, or not subject to qualification; used of rights, orders, titles, privileges, and obligations that operate without conditions attaching to a qualified or provisional counterpart.
- **中文译名:** 绝对的；无条件的；不附限制的
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** General
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-12`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute assignment
- **English definition:** An assignment transferring the assignor’s entire interest in the assigned right or property to the assignee rather than creating merely a partial, conditional, or security interest.
- **中文译名:** 绝对转让；完全让与
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract / property / choses in action
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-13`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute discharge
- **English definition:** A criminal disposition recording guilt but imposing no punishment because punishment is considered unnecessary in the circumstances, unlike a conditional discharge which remains subject to a specified period.
- **中文译名:** 绝对免予处罚；无条件释放
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal sentencing
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-14`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute privilege
- **English definition:** A complete defence to defamation for statements made on occasions the law protects irrespective of malice, including core parliamentary and judicial proceedings and certain legally protected reports or communications.
- **中文译名:** 绝对特权；绝对免责特权（诽谤法）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK; defamation / parliamentary and judicial privilege
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-16`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute right
- **English definition:** A right not subject to limitation or ordinary public-interest balancing. Under the ECHR the prohibition of torture and inhuman or degrading treatment is a leading example; some Convention rights contain an absolute core and a separately qualified aspect.
- **中文译名:** 绝对权利；不得以一般公共利益为由限制的权利
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** ECHR / human rights
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-17`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute theory of sovereign immunity
- **English definition:** Historical doctrine that a foreign state is immune from another state’s courts in virtually all proceedings. Modern state-immunity law generally follows a restrictive approach distinguishing sovereign acts from commercial or private-law activity.
- **中文译名:** 国家绝对豁免理论
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international law; historical / comparative
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-18`
- **Review status:** drafted

## absolute title
- **English definition:** The highest class of registered title to an estate in land under the land-registration system, carrying the statutory effect and state-backed priority attached to that class, subject to interests that bind under land-registration law.
- **中文译名:** 绝对产权；绝对所有权登记等级
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; registered land
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-19`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abstracting electricity
- **English definition:** Dishonestly using, wasting, or diverting electricity without authority. English law treats this as a specific statutory offence because electricity is not treated as ordinary tangible property capable of theft in the usual way.
- **中文译名:** 非法窃用电力；非法抽取／转用电力
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-20`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abstraction of water
- **English definition:** Taking, removing, or impounding water from a river, aquifer, or other source. Water-resources legislation may require a licence and may impose quantity, environmental, and drought-related restrictions.
- **中文译名:** 取水；水资源抽取
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; environmental / water law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-21`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abstract of title
- **English definition:** A summary or collection of documents showing the chain and quality of a person’s title to land or another property interest so that another party can investigate title; its form differs between registered and unregistered land.
- **中文译名:** 产权摘要；权属文件摘要
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Conveyancing; England and Wales
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-22`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abus de droit
- **English definition:** French for “abuse of right”. A civil-law and EU-law concept under which reliance on a legal right or benefit may be denied where it is invoked through an artificial or abusive arrangement contrary to the purpose of the governing rule.
- **中文译名:** 权利滥用
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Civil law; EU law; comparative law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4820`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abuse of a dominant position
- **English definition:** Conduct by a dominant undertaking that exploits customers or excludes competitors in a manner prohibited by competition law. Dominance itself is not unlawful; abuse may include unfair conditions, exclusionary refusals to supply, tying, discrimination, or predatory pricing.
- **中文译名:** 滥用市场支配地位
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK competition law; EU competition law where applicable
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-23`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abuse of a position of trust
- **English definition:** Sexual conduct made criminal because an adult occupies a legally defined position of trust in relation to a person under 18, for example through care, education, or another protected relationship.
- **中文译名:** 滥用信任地位；利用特殊信任关系实施性行为
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; sexual offences
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-24`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abuse of process
- **English definition:** Misuse of judicial procedure for a purpose or in a manner inconsistent with the proper administration of justice. Courts may stay, strike out, or otherwise control proceedings that are oppressive, duplicative, dishonest, or fundamentally unfair.
- **中文译名:** 滥用诉讼程序；滥用司法程序
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Common law; civil and criminal procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-25`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abusive behaviour
- **English definition:** Threatening or abusive words or conduct that may attract criminal or civil consequences where the elements of a public-order, harassment, domestic-abuse, or related rule are satisfied.
- **中文译名:** 辱骂性／威胁性行为；侮辱性行为
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK; context-dependent public-order terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-26`
- **Review status:** drafted

## abutter
- **English definition:** An owner or occupier of land directly adjoining another parcel, road, watercourse, or boundary.
- **中文译名:** 相邻土地所有人；毗邻业主

## ABWOR

## ACAS

- **中文译名:** 英国咨询、调解与仲裁服务局

## acceleration
- **English definition:** Bringing forward the time at which a future interest, right, obligation, or payment becomes possessory or enforceable; for example a remainder may take effect earlier when the preceding interest ends unexpectedly.
- **中文译名:** 提前生效；权利加速到期／取得
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Property / succession / contract

## acceptance
- **English definition:** Unqualified assent to the terms of an offer, made in a legally effective manner and with intention to conclude the agreement. Unless an exception applies, acceptance must be communicated and must correspond with the offer rather than introduce new terms.
- **中文译名:** 承诺；接受要约
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-30`

## acceptance of a bill
- **English definition:** The drawee’s signed assent on a bill of exchange to the drawer’s order to pay. Valid acceptance makes the drawee the acceptor and primarily liable on the bill according to its terms.
- **中文译名:** 汇票承兑

- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-31`
- **Review status:** drafted

## acceptance supra protest
- **English definition:** Also **acceptance for honour**: acceptance of a dishonoured bill by a person not already liable on it, undertaken for the honour of the drawer or an endorser and subject to bills-of-exchange formalities.
- **中文译名:** 参加承兑；荣誉承兑

## accession
- **English definition:** 1. In treaty law, the formal act by which a state becomes bound by a treaty without having signed at the original signing stage. 2. Succession to an office or status, especially a monarch’s accession to the throne. 3. In property law, the principle by which additions, products, or things inseparably attached to property follow ownership of the principal property.
- **中文译名:** 1. 加入（条约） 2. 即位；继位 3. 添附；附合

## access land

## accessory
- **English definition:** 1. A person who intentionally assists, encourages, counsels, or procures commission of an offence without necessarily committing every element as principal. 2. In older usage, one who assists an offender after the offence; modern law generally deals with such conduct through distinct offences.

## accessory liability in breach of trust
- **English definition:** Personal equitable liability imposed on a person who dishonestly assists a trustee or fiduciary in committing a breach of trust or fiduciary duty. Receipt of trust property is not required; liability rests on dishonest assistance.
- **中文译名:** 协助违反信托义务的责任；不诚实协助责任
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Equity; trusts; common law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-37`
- **Review status:** drafted

## access to neighbouring land
- **English definition:** Statutory mechanism allowing a landowner, in appropriate cases, to obtain a court order permitting temporary access to neighbouring land so that reasonably necessary preservation or maintenance work can be carried out on the applicant’s own land.

## accident

## accommodation bill
