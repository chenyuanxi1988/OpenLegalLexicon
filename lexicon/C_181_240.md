## civil injunction
- **English definition:** Court order requiring a person to do, or not to do, specified acts. In the anti-social-behaviour context, Part 1 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 permits an injunction containing prohibitions and positive requirements where the statutory test is met. Breach is dealt with as contempt rather than as the former criminal offence of breaching an ASBO.
- **中文译名:** 民事禁令；反社会行为民事禁令（特定语境）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; civil / anti-social-behaviour law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4957`
- **Review status:** drafted

## civil liability contribution
- **English definition:** Right under the Civil Liability (Contribution) Act 1978 of a person liable for damage to recover a just and equitable contribution from another person who is liable in respect of the **same damage**. The right can arise across different bases of civil liability, including tort, contract, breach of trust, or otherwise.

## civil partnership
- **English definition:** Statutory legal relationship created by the Civil Partnership Act 2004 and available in England and Wales to both same-sex and opposite-sex couples. It gives partners a legal status and many rights and obligations broadly analogous to marriage. Since 27 February 2023, both parties in England and Wales must be at least 18. Dissolution now operates under the no-fault framework introduced by the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020.

## cognates
- **English definition:** Persons related by blood through descent from a common ancestor. In systems that distinguish different lines of kinship, **cognatic** relationship may be contrasted with narrower agnatic or male-line relationship; the precise significance depends on the succession or family-law system concerned.
- **中文译名:** 血亲；同源亲属
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Family / succession / comparative law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-665`
- **Review status:** drafted

## cohabitants
- **English definition:** Two people who live together in an intimate relationship without being married to or in a civil partnership with each other. England and Wales has no general status of “common-law marriage”: cohabitants may have rights under particular property, trust, child, domestic-abuse, pension, succession, or social-security rules, but they do not automatically acquire the full financial rights of spouses on separation or death.
- **中文译名:** 同居伴侣；非婚同居者
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; family / property / succession law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-666`
- **Review status:** drafted

## co-imperium
- **English definition:** Joint exercise of sovereign or governing authority by two or more states over a territory or entity having a distinct international status. The concept is related to, but not necessarily identical with, a **condominium**, and is principally encountered in historical public-international-law arrangements.
- **中文译名:** 共治；共同统治权；共同主权行使
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international law; historical terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-667`
- **Review status:** drafted

## collaborative law
- **English definition:** Lawyer-assisted dispute-resolution process, used particularly in family cases, in which parties and specially trained lawyers commit to negotiate transparently and constructively without commencing contested court proceedings. A participation agreement ordinarily provides that the collaborative lawyers must withdraw if the process breaks down and either party proceeds to contested litigation, encouraging settlement within the process.
- **中文译名:** 协作式法律程序；合作式家事争议解决
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Family law / alternative dispute resolution
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4834`
- **Review status:** drafted

## collateral
- **English definition:** 1. Property or another asset provided as security for an obligation, often in addition to the debtor’s personal promise. 2. Ancillary or secondary to a principal matter. 3. In kinship terminology, related through a common ancestor but not in a direct line of ascent or descent.
- **中文译名:** 1. 担保品；抵押担保 2. 附带的；次要但相关的 3. 旁系的
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Secured transactions / general legal terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-668`
- **Review status:** drafted

## collateral advantage
- **English definition:** Benefit obtained by a lender or other contracting party in addition to the principal return contemplated by the transaction. In mortgage law a collateral advantage may be unenforceable if it amounts to a clog on the equity of redemption, is unconscionable, or impermissibly continues beyond redemption, although commercially negotiated ancillary benefits are not automatically invalid.
- **中文译名:** 附带利益；抵押交易中的额外利益
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Mortgage / equity / contract law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4410`
- **Review status:** drafted

## collateral benefits
- **English definition:** Payments or advantages received by an injured claimant from a source other than the defendant as a consequence of the injury, such as insurance proceeds, sick pay, pensions, social-security benefits, charitable gifts, or payments from an employer. Whether such benefits reduce damages depends on the applicable legal rule; some are disregarded, some are deducted, and some are dealt with through statutory recoupment.
- **中文译名:** 附带利益；第三方补偿利益
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Tort / damages
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-669`
- **Review status:** drafted

## collateral contract
- **English definition:** Separate contract existing alongside and connected with a principal contract, often formed where one party gives a promise or assurance in consideration of the other entering the main transaction. It can impose an independently enforceable obligation even though the promisor is not a party to the principal contract, provided the ordinary requirements for contractual formation are satisfied.
- **中文译名:** 附属合同；附带合同
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-670`
- **Review status:** drafted

## collective bargaining
- **English definition:** Negotiation between an employer or employers’ association and one or more trade unions concerning pay, hours, working conditions, procedures, and other employment matters. A resulting collective agreement is generally presumed not to be legally enforceable between the collective parties unless it is in writing and expressly states an intention to be legally binding, although particular terms may be incorporated into individual contracts of employment.
- **中文译名:** 集体谈判；劳资集体协商
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK employment / trade-union law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-672`
- **Review status:** drafted
