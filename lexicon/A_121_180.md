## Admiralty law
- **English definition:** The body of law governing maritime navigation, ships, carriage by sea, collisions, salvage, maritime liens, limitation of liability, and related claims; often used interchangeably with **maritime law**.
- **中文译名:** 海事法；海商法（依语境）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Maritime / admiralty law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4937`
- **Review status:** drafted

## admissibility of evidence
- **English definition:** The rules determining whether particular evidence may lawfully be received and considered by a court or tribunal. Relevance is fundamental, but relevant evidence may still be excluded by evidential, procedural, privilege, fairness, or public-policy rules.
- **中文译名:** 证据可采性；证据准入资格
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Evidence / procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-108`

## adultery
- **English definition:** Traditionally, voluntary sexual intercourse between a married person and someone other than that person’s spouse.
