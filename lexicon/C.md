# OpenLegalLexicon — C

> Oxford 10e source order. English definitions are independently drafted; Chinese gives term-level translations by sense. Current-law changes since 2022 are reflected where material.

## Cabinet
- **English definition:** The senior group of government ministers, chaired by the Prime Minister, through which major government policy and collective executive decisions are coordinated. The Cabinet is principally a constitutional-convention institution rather than a body created by a single founding statute, and its members are subject to collective ministerial responsibility.
- **中文译名:** 内阁
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK constitutional law / government
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-461`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Cabinet Office
- **English definition:** A UK government department that supports the Prime Minister and Cabinet, coordinates collective government and cross-departmental work, and performs central corporate-government functions, in partnership with HM Treasury in several areas.
- **中文译名:** 英国内阁办公室
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK government / public administration
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4829`
- **Review status:** drafted

## cabotage
- **English definition:** Transport of goods or passengers between two points within one state by a carrier established or registered in another state. Cabotage rights are commonly restricted by domestic law or international transport agreements; EU law provides a specific regime for carriers operating temporarily within another member state, while UK–EU operations are now governed by post-Brexit arrangements.
- **中文译名:** 沿海运输；境内运输；外国承运人境内运输
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Transport / EU / international trade law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-462`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Calderbank letter
- **English definition:** A settlement offer made on a **without prejudice save as to costs** basis, so that its terms are ordinarily withheld from the court deciding the merits but may later be considered when costs are determined. The expression derives from *Calderbank v Calderbank* and remains relevant where Part 36 does not apply or where parties choose a non-Part-36 costs offer, including in family proceedings.
- **中文译名:** Calderbank 和解函；“不损害实体权利但可用于费用问题”的和解要约
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; civil / family procedure and costs
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-465`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Calvo clause
- **English definition:** A clause, historically associated with Latin American state contracts and investment arrangements, by which a foreign party agrees to use local law and domestic remedies and purports to renounce diplomatic intervention by its home state. Such a clause cannot necessarily extinguish the home state’s independent right of diplomatic protection under international law.
- **中文译名:** 卡尔沃条款；放弃外交保护条款
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international / investment law; historical doctrine
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-468`

## cancellation

Exercise of a statutory or contractual right to withdraw from a contract, especially a consumer contract, within a prescribed period or on specified grounds. Cancellation should be distinguished from termination for future performance and from rescission where those concepts have different legal effects.
- **中文译名:** 撤销；取消；合同撤回／解除（依具体制度）

## cannabis

## cannon-shot rule
- **English definition:** Historical rule associating a coastal state’s territorial sea with the effective range of shore-based cannon, conventionally expressed as a three-nautical-mile limit. It has been superseded by modern law of the sea, under which UNCLOS permits a territorial sea of up to 12 nautical miles from the applicable baseline.
- **中文译名:** 炮程规则；三海里领海规则（历史）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** International law of the sea; historical

## capacity of a child in criminal law
- **English definition:** The legal ability of a child to incur criminal responsibility. In England and Wales, a child under 10 cannot be criminally responsible; for children aged 10 and over the former rebuttable presumption of **doli incapax** was abolished by section 34 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998. Other UK jurisdictions have their own age-of-responsibility rules.
- **中文译名:** 儿童刑事责任能力；未成年人刑事责任能力

## capacity to consent to medical treatment
- **English definition:** Ability to make a legally effective decision about medical treatment. In England and Wales, persons aged 16 or 17 are generally presumed capable of consenting; a child under 16 may consent if sufficiently mature and understanding under the **Gillick competence** principle. For persons within the Mental Capacity Act 2005, capacity is decision-specific and assessed under the statutory test.
- **中文译名:** 医疗同意能力；同意接受医疗的能力

## capacity to contract
- **English definition:** Legal ability to enter a binding contract. Companies generally have legal capacity to contract, although constitutional and statutory restrictions may affect internal authority or particular transactions.

## casus foederis
- **English definition:** Latin expression for the event or circumstance that triggers an ally’s obligation to provide assistance under a treaty of alliance or mutual-defence agreement. Whether the triggering event has occurred depends on the wording and interpretation of the treaty.
- **中文译名:** 联盟义务触发事由；同盟条约适用事件
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international / treaty law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4637`
- **Review status:** drafted

## casus omissus
- **English definition:** Latin expression for a case or circumstance omitted from the wording of a legal rule or statute. The concept cautions courts against supplying an omitted provision merely because it appears desirable; whether an apparent gap can be resolved by interpretation depends on the text, context, purpose, and applicable interpretive principles.
- **中文译名:** 法律遗漏；未规定情形
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Statutory interpretation / legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-509`
- **Review status:** drafted

## catching bargain
- **English definition:** Traditional equitable expression for an improvident or unconscionable transaction obtained by taking advantage of another person’s weakness, need, lack of bargaining power, or expectant interest, especially at a gross undervalue. Equity may set aside or restrict enforcement of such a bargain where the applicable unconscionability principles are satisfied.
- **中文译名:** 显失公平交易；乘人之危的苛刻交易
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Equity / contract law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-510`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causa causans
- **English definition:** Latin expression referring to the operative, effective, or legally significant cause of an event, sometimes contrasted with a merely necessary background condition. It appears mainly in older discussions of causation; modern courts generally analyse factual and legal causation directly rather than treating the phrase as an independent test.
- **中文译名:** 直接有效原因；主要致因
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Causation / legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4638`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causa sine qua non
- **English definition:** Latin for a necessary condition: a cause without which the result would not have occurred. In legal causation it corresponds broadly to the **but-for** inquiry into factual causation, although satisfying that inquiry does not by itself establish legal responsibility.
- **中文译名:** 必要条件原因；不可或缺原因
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Causation / legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4399`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causation
- **English definition:** The legal connection between conduct and a result. Analysis commonly distinguishes **factual causation**, often tested by asking whether the result would have occurred but for the conduct, from **legal causation**, which asks whether the connection is sufficiently direct and attributable for liability. Intervening acts, multiple causes, remoteness, and special evidential rules may alter the analysis.
- **中文译名:** 因果关系；法律因果关系
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Tort / criminal / damages law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-513`
- **Review status:** drafted

## cause
- **English definition:** 1. A lawsuit, legal proceeding, or matter before a court, especially in traditional procedural terminology. 2. An act, event, or condition that contributes to a legally relevant result; see **causation**.
- **中文译名:** 1. 诉讼；案件 2. 原因；致因
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Procedure / causation
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-514`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Cause Book
- **English definition:** Traditional court register recording the commencement of proceedings and specified subsequent procedural events. The expression is associated particularly with the Central Office of the Senior Courts; modern court administration may maintain equivalent information electronically rather than in a physical book.
- **中文译名:** 诉讼登记簿；案件立案登记册
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; court administration; traditional terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-515`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Cause List
- **English definition:** A court’s published list of cases, applications, hearings, or trials scheduled to be heard on a particular day or during a stated period. Modern cause lists are commonly published electronically as well as made available through the court and may include warnings or provisional listings.
- **中文译名:** 开庭案件表；庭审排期表；案件日程表
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Court administration / procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-516`
