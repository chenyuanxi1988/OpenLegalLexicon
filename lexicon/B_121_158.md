## Brexit
- **English definition:** The United Kingdom’s withdrawal from the European Union. Following the 2016 referendum, the UK notified withdrawal under Article 50 TEU in 2017, left the EU on 31 January 2020, and completed the transition period on 31 December 2020. The term also refers more broadly to the legal and institutional changes arising from that withdrawal.
- **中文译名:** 英国脱欧；英国退出欧盟
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK / EU constitutional and public law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4828`
- **Review status:** drafted

## bribery
- **English definition:** Offering, promising, giving, requesting, agreeing to receive, or accepting a financial or other advantage in circumstances prohibited by the Bribery Act 2010. The Act also creates offences concerning bribery of foreign public officials and failure by a commercial organisation to prevent bribery by associated persons, subject to the statutory defence.
- **中文译名:** 贿赂；行贿或受贿
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK criminal / corporate compliance law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-429`
- **Review status:** drafted

## bridleway
- **English definition:** A highway or public right of way over which the public is entitled to pass on foot and on horseback or while leading a horse. Other statutory rights, including limited cycling rights, may also apply, but a bridleway is legally distinct from a carriageway.
- **中文译名:** 骑马道；公共骑马通行道
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; highways / rights of way
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-430`
- **Review status:** drafted

## British citizenship
- **English definition:** The principal form of British nationality under the British Nationality Act 1981. A British citizen ordinarily has the right of abode in the United Kingdom and may obtain a British citizen passport. Citizenship may arise by birth, descent, registration, or naturalisation according to statutory conditions; birth in the UK alone does not always confer citizenship.
- **中文译名:** 英国公民身份；英国国籍（British citizenship）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK nationality law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-432`
- **Review status:** drafted

- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-433`

## British National (Overseas)
- **English definition:** A form of British nationality connected with Hong Kong. Eligible British dependent territories citizens could register for British National (Overseas), or BN(O), status before 1 July 1997; it cannot now be newly acquired by registration. BN(O) status does not itself give an automatic right of abode in the UK, although a separate BN(O) immigration route exists.
- **中文译名:** 英国国民（海外）；BN(O)
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK nationality / Hong Kong-related status
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-434`
- **Review status:** drafted

## British Overseas citizenship
- **English definition:** A residual form of British nationality created by the British Nationality Act 1981, principally for persons who were citizens of the United Kingdom and Colonies but did not become British citizens or British overseas territories citizens when the Act took effect. It generally does not carry an automatic right of abode in the UK, and new acquisition is limited.
- **中文译名:** 英国海外公民身份；英国海外公民国籍
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK nationality law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-435`
- **Review status:** drafted

## British Overseas Territories citizenship
- **English definition:** A form of British nationality based on a qualifying connection with a British Overseas Territory. It was known as British Dependent Territories citizenship before 2002. Many holders connected with qualifying territories also became British citizens in 2002, but the two statuses remain legally distinct.
- **中文译名:** 英国海外领土公民身份；英国海外领土公民国籍
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK nationality / overseas territories
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-436`
- **Review status:** drafted

## British protected person
- **English definition:** A limited form of British nationality status arising principally from historical connections with former British protectorates, protected states, or trust territories. A British protected person may hold a British passport and receive consular protection but generally has no automatic right to live or work in the UK merely by virtue of that status.
- **中文译名:** 英国保护人士；英国受保护人
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK nationality law; historical imperial connections
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-437`
- **Review status:** drafted

## British subject
- **English definition:** A residual British nationality status under the British Nationality Act 1981, now held by relatively few people and derived principally from historical categories predating modern British citizenship. It is not synonymous with British citizen, and an automatic right of abode does not attach to every British subject.
- **中文译名:** 英籍臣民；英国臣民身份（现为有限的法定国籍身份）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK nationality law; historical / residual status
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-438`
- **Review status:** drafted

- **中文译名:** 妓院；卖淫场所

## Brussels Convention
- **English definition:** The 1968 Convention on jurisdiction and the enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters, originally concluded between European Community states. It established common jurisdiction and judgment-enforcement rules and was later superseded for most purposes by the Brussels I regime. For the UK it now has only limited residual or transitional relevance, including certain territories and older judgments.
- **中文译名:** 《布鲁塞尔公约》（1968 年民商事管辖权与判决执行公约）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** European private international law; largely historical / residual
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-441`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Bryan Treaties

They provided for unresolved international disputes to be submitted to permanent commissions of inquiry and generally required the parties to refrain from hostilities while the commission investigated and reported.

## buggery
- **English definition:** Historical criminal-law term for specified forms of anal intercourse and bestiality. The former offence of buggery in England and Wales was repealed by the Sexual Offences Act 2003. Conduct formerly described by the term may now fall within modern offences such as rape, other non-consensual sexual offences, or sexual activity with an animal, depending on the facts.
- **中文译名:** 鸡奸（旧法律术语）；兽交（旧罪名语境）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; historical sexual-offence terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-444`

## bugging
- **English definition:** Covert use of a listening, recording, or monitoring device to obtain communications or activity without the subject’s awareness. Depending on who conducts it and how, bugging may be regulated by investigatory-powers legislation, privacy and data-protection law, criminal law, or evidential rules.
- **中文译名:** 窃听；秘密监听

## building lease

Rights in the completed buildings and their treatment at the end of the lease depend on the lease and the general law of land and fixtures.

## building preservation notice
- **English definition:** A temporary notice protecting a building that appears to be of special architectural or historic interest while permanent listing is considered. In England, once validly served, the building is treated for relevant purposes as if listed and the notice normally remains effective for up to six months unless superseded or brought to an end earlier. Wales now has separate historic-environment legislation.
- **中文译名:** 建筑保护通知；临时列级保护通知
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England; planning / listed buildings
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-447`

- **中文译名:** 建筑开发共同限制方案；地块共同契约方案

## Bullock order
- **English definition:** Costs order used where a claimant reasonably sues two or more defendants, succeeds against one, and fails against another. The claimant is ordered to pay the successful defendant’s costs but may recover those costs from the unsuccessful defendant as part of the claimant’s own costs. It differs from a Sanderson order, under which the unsuccessful defendant pays the successful defendant directly.
- **中文译名:** Bullock 费用令；布洛克诉讼费用命令
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; civil costs
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-450`
- **Review status:** drafted

## burden of proof
- **English definition:** Responsibility for establishing a fact or issue in legal proceedings. The **legal or persuasive burden** determines which party loses on an issue if the required standard is not met; the **evidential burden** concerns producing sufficient evidence to make the issue properly arguable. The applicable party and standard depend on the type of proceeding and issue.
- **中文译名:** 证明责任；举证责任
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Evidence / civil and criminal procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-451`
- **Review status:** drafted

## burglary
- **English definition:** Under section 9 of the Theft Act 1968, an offence committed where a person enters a building or part of a building as a trespasser with intent to steal, inflict grievous bodily harm, or unlawfully damage property, or, having entered as a trespasser, steals or attempts to steal or inflicts or attempts to inflict grievous bodily harm. An inhabited vehicle or vessel can count as a building for this purpose.
- **中文译名:** 入室盗窃罪；侵入建筑物盗窃／伤害罪
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-452`
- **Review status:** drafted

## business asset
- **English definition:** An asset used, held, or disposed of in connection with a business. In UK tax law the expression may have a specialised statutory meaning for a particular relief, including Business Asset Disposal Relief, and the fact that an asset is owned by a business does not by itself establish eligibility for any tax relief.

## business efficacy test
- **English definition:** Test used when considering whether a term should be implied into a contract as a matter of fact. The proposed term must be necessary to make the contract work in the relevant commercial sense, not merely fair or reasonable; modern authority treats necessity as a strict requirement considered in the context of the express agreement.
- **中文译名:** 商业效力测试；商业可行性默示条款测试
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract law / implied terms
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4948`
- **Review status:** drafted

## business liability
- **English definition:** Liability arising from acts or omissions occurring in the course of a business, or from occupation of premises used for business purposes. The expression is particularly important in the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977, which regulates contractual attempts to exclude or restrict certain business liabilities; consumer contracts are principally governed by the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
- **中文译名:** 营业责任；商业经营责任
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK contract / tort / exclusion clauses
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-455`

## business name
- **English definition:** A name under which an individual, partnership, LLP, or company carries on business, distinct from the legal person’s own registered or personal name where applicable. UK law restricts certain names and requires specified identity disclosures, and use of a business name does not by itself create a separate legal entity or trade-mark right.
- **中文译名:** 商号；营业名称；商业名称

## business property relief
- **English definition:** Traditional name for **Business Relief**, an inheritance-tax relief reducing the taxable value of qualifying business property. The rate and conditions depend on the property and the statutory rules. For deaths on or after 6 April 2026, 100% agricultural and business relief is subject to a combined £2.5 million allowance, with qualifying value above that allowance generally receiving 50% relief; some categories receive 50% relief in any event.
- **中文译名:** 商业财产减免；遗产税商业资产减免（现称 Business Relief）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK inheritance tax; current rules from 6 April 2026
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-457`
- **Review status:** drafted

## business tenancy
- **English definition:** A tenancy of premises occupied by the tenant for the purposes of a business. Under Part II of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, many qualifying business tenancies have statutory security of tenure and a right to seek a new tenancy when the contractual term ends, unless the tenancy was validly contracted out or the landlord establishes a statutory ground for refusal.
- **中文译名:** 商业租赁；营业场所租约
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; landlord and tenant law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-458`
- **Review status:** drafted

## “but for” test
- **English definition:** Basic test of factual causation asking whether the relevant harm or result would have occurred **but for** the defendant’s act or omission. If the harm would have occurred in the same way anyway, factual causation will ordinarily not be established, subject to special rules for multiple, cumulative, or scientifically uncertain causes.
- **中文译名:** “若非”测试；but-for 因果关系测试
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Tort / criminal law / causation
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4395`
- **Review status:** drafted

## byelaw

- **中文译名:** 地方附例；地方规章；自治条例
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK local government / delegated legislation
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-460`
