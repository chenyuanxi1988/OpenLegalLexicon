## cause of action
- **English definition:** The set of material facts that, if proved, entitles a claimant to a legal remedy against a defendant. Different causes of action have different constituent elements, limitation periods, and available remedies.
- **中文译名:** 诉因；请求权基础所需事实
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Civil procedure / substantive law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-517`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causing a child to watch a sexual act
- **English definition:** An offence under section 12 of the Sexual Offences Act 2003 committed where, for sexual gratification, a person intentionally causes a child under 16 to watch another person engaging in sexual activity or to look at an image of sexual activity. The relevance of a mistaken belief about age depends on whether the child is under 13 or aged 13–15 and on the statutory conditions.
- **中文译名:** 致使儿童观看性行为罪
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; sexual offences
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-518`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causing death by careless driving when under the influence of drink
- **English definition:** The Road Traffic Act 1988 offence, commonly expressed as **causing death by careless driving when under the influence of drink or drugs**, committed where careless driving causes death and the driver also satisfies one of the statutory drink-, drug-, or testing-related conditions in section 3A.
- **中文译名:** 酒驾／毒驾状态下疏忽驾驶致人死亡罪
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Great Britain; road traffic / criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-519`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causing death by careless
- **English definition:** Shortened source heading for **causing death by careless or inconsiderate driving**, the offence under section 2B of the Road Traffic Act 1988 where a person causes another’s death by driving without due care and attention or without reasonable consideration for other road users.
- **中文译名:** 疏忽或不顾他人驾驶致人死亡罪
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Great Britain; road traffic / criminal law

## causing death by dangerous driving
- **English definition:** Offence under section 1 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 committed where a person causes another’s death by driving dangerously. “Dangerous” is assessed under the statutory objective standard concerning driving falling far below that expected of a competent and careful driver and obvious danger.
- **中文译名:** 危险驾驶致人死亡罪
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Great Britain; road traffic / criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-520`
- **Review status:** drafted

## causing loss by unlawful means
- **English definition:** Economic tort committed where the defendant intentionally causes loss to the claimant by unlawfully interfering with a third party’s freedom to deal with the claimant. The relevant unlawful means must ordinarily be conduct actionable by the third party, subject to the limits developed in case law.
- **中文译名:** 以非法手段致人损失；非法手段经济侵权
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Economic torts
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4401`
- **Review status:** drafted

## caution
- **English definition:** 1. The formal warning given under PACE before a suspect is questioned about an offence, drawing attention to the right to silence and possible evidential consequences. 2. A **simple caution**, an out-of-court criminal disposal accepted by an offender who admits the offence and meets the applicable criteria. 3. Historically in registered land, a caution against dealings; that mechanism was replaced by the Land Registration Act 2002 notice system.
- **中文译名:** 1. 警方警告语；权利告知 2. 简易警告处分 3. 土地登记警示（旧制）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; criminal procedure / land law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-521`
- **Review status:** drafted

## caution against first registration
- **English definition:** Entry that may be lodged in relation to unregistered land by a person claiming a qualifying interest, requiring the Land Registry to notify the cautioner if an application for first registration is made so that the cautioner can object where appropriate. It does not itself register title to the land.
- **中文译名:** 首次登记警示；针对土地首次登记的警告登记
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; land registration
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-522`
- **Review status:** drafted

## caveat
- **English definition:** A formal notice requiring that a specified legal step not be taken without first notifying the person lodging the notice. In probate practice, a caveat entered in the probate registry prevents a grant of representation from issuing in the ordinary way until the caveat is resolved, withdrawn, or otherwise dealt with.
- **中文译名:** 异议警示；停止办理通知；遗嘱认证异议备案
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Probate / general procedural terminology
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-523`
- **Review status:** drafted

## caveat emptor
- **English definition:** Latin for **let the buyer beware**, the traditional common-law principle that a purchaser should protect itself as to the quality or suitability of what is bought. Modern sale-of-goods and consumer legislation substantially qualifies the maxim by implying statutory standards and remedies.
- **中文译名:** 买者自慎；买方自负查验责任原则
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Sale of goods / consumer / contract law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-525`
- **Review status:** drafted

## caveat subscriptor
- **English definition:** Latin for **let the signer beware**, expressing the principle that a person who signs a document is generally expected to understand and accept its legal effect, subject to doctrines such as misrepresentation, mistake, non est factum, undue influence, and statutory controls.
- **中文译名:** 签署者自慎；签字人须自负其责
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract / legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-526`
- **Review status:** drafted

## CBO
- **English definition:** Abbreviation for **Criminal Behaviour Order**, an order that may be made following conviction where the statutory test concerning anti-social behaviour and prevention of further such behaviour is met. It can prohibit specified conduct and impose positive requirements, and breach is a separate criminal offence.

## CCGs

CCGs were abolished on 1 July 2022 and their principal commissioning functions transferred to **Integrated Care Boards (ICBs)**.

## CCRC

## CE
- **English definition:** **CE marking** indicates that a product subject to relevant EU product legislation is declared or assessed as meeting the applicable EU conformity requirements. In Great Britain, CE-marked products continue to be recognized for many regulated product sectors alongside UKCA under current sector-specific rules, while other sectors, including medical devices, have separate transitional regimes. Northern Ireland remains subject to distinct EU-related marking rules.
- **中文译名:** CE 标志；欧盟合格认证标志

## certainty
- **English definition:** Requirement that a contract be sufficiently definite for the court to identify the parties’ obligations and give legal effect to their agreement. A contract is not invalid merely because every detail is unstated: terms may be ascertainable from objective standards, machinery agreed by the parties, trade usage, or rules of law, but the court will not create a bargain the parties never made.
- **中文译名:** 确定性；合同条款明确性
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4830`
- **Review status:** drafted

## certificates of readiness

Modern Crown Court case management is governed by the Criminal Procedure Rules and current listing and trial-preparation directions, so the historical form should not be assumed to remain the standard procedure.

## cessante ratione legis, cessat lex ipsa
