## Agricultural Land and Drainage

## agricultural property relief
- **English definition:** Inheritance-tax relief for qualifying agricultural property. From 6 April 2026, 100% Agricultural Property Relief and Business Property Relief share a combined £2.5 million 100%-relief allowance for an individual, with qualifying value above the available allowance generally receiving 50% relief; separate rules govern trusts, lifetime transfers, and transferable unused allowance between spouses or civil partners.
- **中文译名:** 农业财产遗产税减免；农业财产减免（APR）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK inheritance tax
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-160`
- **Review status:** drafted; 6 April 2026 rules updated
