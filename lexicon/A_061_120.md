# OpenLegalLexicon — A — entries 61–120

> Editorial batch. English definitions are independently drafted. Dynamic institutional terminology has been updated where necessary.

## acknowledgment
- **English definition:** 1. An admission or recognition that a debt, claim, fact, or obligation exists. 2. Formal confirmation by a person that a signature or document is theirs or was executed by them.
- **中文译名:** 1. 承认；确认 2. 签署确认；签名确认
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** General; obligations / evidence / conveyancing
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-58`
- **Review status:** drafted

## acknowledgment and undertaking
- **English definition:** In conveyancing, a title-document arrangement under which the holder acknowledges another person’s right to inspect and obtain copies of relevant deeds and undertakes to keep those deeds safe.
- **中文译名:** 契据查阅确认与保管承诺；产权文件确认与承诺
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; conveyancing, principally unregistered land
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-59`
- **Review status:** drafted

## acknowledgment of service
- **English definition:** A procedural document by which a defendant confirms receipt of originating process and indicates an intention to respond, contest jurisdiction, or take another procedural step permitted by the rules.
- **中文译名:** 送达确认书；送达回执确认
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; civil procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-60`
- **Review status:** drafted

## a coelo usque ad centrum
- **English definition:** Latin phrase meaning “from the heavens to the centre of the earth”, associated with the traditional maxim describing the vertical extent of landownership. Modern law substantially qualifies any literal reading of the maxim.
- **中文译名:** 从天穹直至地心；传统土地所有权垂直范围表述
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Legal Latin; property law; historical maxim
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-61`
- **Review status:** drafted

## ACP Group

- **中文译名:** 非洲、加勒比和太平洋国家集团（现称非洲、加勒比和太平洋国家组织，OACPS）

- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-62`
- **Review status:** drafted; current-name update checked

## acquiescence
- **English definition:** Express or implied acceptance of, or failure to object to, another person’s conduct in circumstances where the law treats that conduct as consent, waiver, or a bar to later objection. Mere knowledge is not necessarily acquiescence.
- **中文译名:** 默许；默认；默示同意
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Equity / contract / tort / procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-63`
- **Review status:** drafted

- **Review status:** drafted

## acquis communautaire

## action

## actio personalis moritur cum persona
- **English definition:** Latin maxim meaning “a personal action dies with the person”. Historically it caused some personal claims to end on the death of claimant or defendant; legislation has largely displaced the rule, although limited remnants remain in particular causes of action.
- **中文译名:** 人身诉权随本人死亡而消灭；人亡诉灭（历史格言）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Common law; historical tort / succession doctrine
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4363`
- **Review status:** drafted

## actio popularis
- **English definition:** An action brought to protect a public or community-wide legal interest rather than a right peculiar to the claimant. In international law, standing of this kind is not generally presumed and ordinarily requires a specific legal basis.
- **中文译名:** 公益诉讼；民众诉讼（国际法／罗马法术语）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international law / comparative law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4619`
- **Review status:** drafted

## actio quanti minoris
- **English definition:** A price-reduction remedy of Roman and civil-law origin allowing a buyer to retain defective goods while claiming an appropriate reduction in price reflecting the defect or reduced value.
- **中文译名:** 减价之诉；瑕疵商品价款减额请求
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Roman / civil law; comparative sales law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4364`
- **Review status:** drafted

## active trust
- **English definition:** A trust under which the trustee has continuing management, protective, discretionary, or other substantive duties beyond merely holding property for immediate transfer to the beneficiary.
- **中文译名:** 积极信托；有管理职责的信托
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Trusts

## Activities of Transnational Corporations Treaty
- **English definition:** A shorthand description for the UN process, begun under Human Rights Council Resolution 26/9, to elaborate a legally binding international instrument concerning transnational corporations and other business enterprises with respect to human rights. It remains a treaty-negotiation process rather than a generally operative treaty.
- **中文译名:** 跨国公司及其他工商企业与人权活动条约（拟议中的联合国法律约束性文书）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** International human-rights law; treaty negotiation
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4936`
- **Review status:** drafted; current-status wording updated

## activity directions and conditions
- **English definition:** Directions or conditions attached to a child-arrangements order requiring a person, typically a parent, to participate in an activity intended to establish, maintain, or improve that person’s or another person’s involvement in the child’s life, such as counselling or parenting work.
- **中文译名:** 活动指示与条件；子女安排令中的参与活动要求
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; family law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4821`
- **Review status:** drafted

## activity requirement
- **English definition:** A requirement that may form part of a youth rehabilitation order, directing the young offender to participate in specified activities or activities directed by the responsible officer, including rehabilitative or reparative work.
- **中文译名:** 活动要求；青少年矫正令中的指定活动要求
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; youth sentencing
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-71`
- **Review status:** drafted

## act of a stranger
- **English definition:** An independent and unforeseeable act of a third person over whom the defendant has no control, capable in some contexts of breaking responsibility for nuisance or strict-liability damage.
- **中文译名:** 第三人行为；陌生人行为

- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-4365`

## act of God
- **English definition:** An extraordinary natural event that could not reasonably have been foreseen or guarded against by ordinary human care. Depending on the governing rule or contract, it may exclude liability where the event is the effective cause of the loss.
- **中文译名:** 天灾；不可抗力的自然事件
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract / tort / carriage / insurance
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-72`
- **Review status:** drafted

## Act of Parliament

## act of state
- **English definition:** A doctrine concerning certain sovereign or governmental acts, especially acts of a foreign state within its own territory, whose validity or lawfulness domestic courts may decline or be unable to adjudicate. Its scope is limited and depends on the precise public- and private-law context.
- **中文译名:** 国家行为；国家行为原则
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Public international law / conflict of laws / constitutional law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-74`
- **Review status:** drafted

## actual bodily harm

## actual military service

## actual notice

## actual total loss
- **English definition:** In marine insurance, a total loss where the insured subject matter is destroyed, ceases to be the thing insured, or the insured is irretrievably deprived of it, rather than merely facing a constructive total loss.

- **Review status:** drafted

## actus non facit reum nisi mens sit rea
- **English definition:** Latin maxim commonly translated as “an act does not make a person guilty unless the mind is also guilty”. It expresses the general criminal-law requirement that prohibited conduct and the required mental element must coincide, subject to offences imposing strict liability.
- **中文译名:** 无犯意则行为不成罪；行为与罪过相结合原则
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Criminal law; legal Latin
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-80`
- **Review status:** drafted

## actus reus

- **中文译名:** 犯罪行为；犯罪客观要件
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Criminal law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-79`
- **Review status:** drafted

## ademption

## adhesion contract
