## AVC
- **English definition:** Abbreviation for **additional voluntary contribution**, an extra pension contribution voluntarily paid by or for a scheme member in addition to ordinary required contributions in order to increase retirement benefits.
- **中文译名:** 额外自愿养老金缴款；附加自愿缴费（AVC）
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** UK pensions
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-316`
- **Review status:** drafted

## aver
- **English definition:** To allege or state formally that a fact is true, especially in a pleading or other statement of case. The corresponding allegation is an **averment**.
- **中文译名:** 正式主张；陈称为事实
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Pleading / procedure
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-317`
- **Review status:** drafted

## average
- **English definition:** 1. In marine insurance, loss or damage associated with a maritime casualty, including **particular average** and **general average** in their respective contexts. 2. In insurance more generally, a contractual mechanism reducing recovery for a partial loss where property is underinsured in relation to the value on which the policy should have been based.
- **中文译名:** 1. 海损 2. 比例分摊／不足额保险比例赔付
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Marine insurance / insurance
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-318`
- **Review status:** drafted

## avoid
- **English definition:** To rescind, set aside, cancel, or deprive a voidable transaction, contract, disposition, or legal act of effect by exercising a legal right or obtaining the required judicial relief.
- **中文译名:** 撤销；使失效；解除可撤销法律行为
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Contract / equity / insolvency
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-320`
- **Review status:** drafted

## avoidance of disposition order
- **English definition:** A family-court order preventing, restraining, or setting aside a disposition made with the intention of defeating another party’s claim for financial relief, subject to statutory protections for transactions made for value to persons acting in good faith and without notice of the defeating purpose.
- **中文译名:** 撤销规避财产处分令；防止逃避婚姻财产救济的处分撤销令
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** England and Wales; family financial remedies
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-321`
- **Review status:** drafted

## avulsion
- **English definition:** Sudden and perceptible removal or relocation of land, or abrupt change in a river channel, caused by natural forces. Unlike gradual accretion, avulsion generally does not automatically shift an established property or international boundary merely because the watercourse has suddenly moved.
- **中文译名:** 突变移位；河道骤变；土地突失／突增
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Property law / international boundary law
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-322`
- **Review status:** drafted

## award
- **English definition:** 1. The binding decision of an arbitral tribunal resolving all or part of a dispute submitted to arbitration. 2. More generally, a sum, remedy, or benefit formally granted by a court, tribunal, adjudicator, or other authorized decision-maker.
- **中文译名:** 1. 仲裁裁决 2. 裁定给付；裁决金额
- **Jurisdiction / scope:** Arbitration / dispute resolution
- **Source:** Oxford 10e — `acref-9780192897497-e-323`
- **Review status:** drafted
